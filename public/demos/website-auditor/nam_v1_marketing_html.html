The Partner Model

Your SPOC isn't a logistics owner. They're a co-facilitator.

NAM is engineered as a partnership — not a vendor-delivered training. A Single Point of Contact (SPOC) from your organisation is embedded as an active co-facilitator of every cohort: contextualising frameworks to your culture, mentoring nominees, and feeding back monthly to the Northstar Learning team.

This is what HR leaders in the World Café said matters most: "learning as a system" — journals, micro-learning slots, peer forums, role-modelling by leaders, and safe experimentation pods with governance.

One programme. Three contexts.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about the Modern Manager Programme.

What is the eligibility criteria for the programme?

The programme is designed for professionals with a minimum of 3 years of work experience and a graduate degree from a recognized university. It is ideal for newly-promoted managers, team leads, or those transitioning into leadership roles.

What are the technical and platform requirements?

The programme is hosted on the Northstar Learning learning platform (built on Open edX). We recommend using a laptop or desktop computer with a modern browser such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari, along with a stable high-speed internet connection.

How does the corporate nomination process work?

Organizations can nominate a cohort of managers. A dedicated Single Point of Contact (SPOC) from the nominating company co-facilitates the corporate context track, aligning the capstone projects to actual business challenges.

Is there an installment option for the fee?

Yes, the program fee of ₹ 1,50,000 + 18% GST can be paid in two equal installments: the first installment at the time of enrollment, and the second installment prior to the mid-term campus immersion.

What certificate will I receive upon completion?

Upon successfully completing all weekly assessments and the final Action Learning Capstone Project, you will receive a Certificate of Completion in Modern Management from Northstar Learning.
