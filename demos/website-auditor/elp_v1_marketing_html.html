Capstone · Action Learning Project

Don't write a paper.
Move a number.

The Action Learning Project (ALP) runs across all seven months, not bolted on at the end. You pick a real problem from your own organisation, and the programme bends around it.

Faculty mentor you. Cohort peers stress-test your hypotheses. You ship a recommendation, a prototype, or a measurable change — and present it at the valedictory in Month 7.

Month 1
Define the problem.
Scope a real business question from your own desk. Faculty review the brief.
Month 3
In-campus stress test.
First on-campus immersion. Pitch the plan. Peers challenge it. Refine.
Month 5
Apply the frameworks.
Themes 3 & 4 wrap. Your project is now informed by customer, strategy and innovation lenses.
Month 7
Ship & defend.
Valedictory on campus. Present to faculty and your sponsoring leader. Walk away with a result, not a binder.

Built for managers about to step up.

If you were promoted in the last two years — or are about to be — ELP closes the gap between the work that got you here and the leadership the next chair demands.

Ideal for:

  • First-time managers who've inherited a team and are learning leadership on the job, often without scaffolding.
  • Emerging leaders (3–8 yrs) in product, operations, technology, marketing or finance — being groomed for cross-functional ownership.
  • Founders & senior ICs moving into general-management roles who need rigorous breadth across leadership, finance, customers, strategy and AI.

You should apply if you have:

  • 3–8 years of professional experience, with at least one year managing people or owning a cross-functional outcome.
  • A graduate degree in any discipline — engineering, management, science, design, or relevant fields.
  • A real problem at your current organisation that you'd like to make the focus of your Action Learning Project.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Answers to common questions about the Emerging Leaders Lab.

What is the difference between ELP and NAM?

ELP (Emerging Leaders Lab) is a longer, 7-month cohort program that covers core management disciplines (finance, strategy, customer metrics) in deeper academic detail with multiple on-campus sessions. NAM is a 6-month corporate-nomination program focused on practical leadership capabilities (coaching, change leadership) designed for cohort-based corporate learning pathways.

Are there on-campus sessions at Northstar Learning?

Yes, ELP 2.0 includes three mandatory on-campus immersion sessions, including a final valedictory in Month 7, where you will present your Action Learning Project directly to faculty and sponsors.

What are the program fees and installment schedules?

The program fee is ₹ 2,45,000 + GST. You can pay in standard installments: ₹ 10,000 as a registration fee, with the remaining fee payable in two equal installments before the first on-campus immersion.

What certificate is awarded?

Participants who successfully complete all course requirements and defend their Capstone project will be awarded the Certificate in Emerging Leadership from Northstar Learning.
